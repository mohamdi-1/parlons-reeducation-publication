# Privacy policy · Parlons rééducation publisher

This small app is used only by the administrator of the Facebook Page « Parlons rééducation » and its linked Instagram account, to schedule and publish the page's own educational posts.

- It does not collect, store or share any personal data about other people.
- It only sends the page's own photos, videos and captions to Facebook and Instagram.
- The access key is kept privately by the administrator (on their computer and as an encrypted repository secret) and is never published.

Data deletion: nothing is stored about users. To remove the app's access, the administrator removes the app in Facebook settings (Settings → Security and login → Business integrations / Apps and websites).

Contact: through the Facebook Page « Parlons rééducation ».
